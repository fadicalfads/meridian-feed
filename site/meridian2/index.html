Glossary
Open price
Where today started trading, at 05:00 UK. ICT traders call it the True Day Open (TDO).
Asia high / low
The top and bottom of overnight trading, 01:00 - 05:00 UK. Orders cluster there.
FVG
Fair value gap: a fast move that leaves a gap between candles. Price often comes back to it. Pastel boxes on the charts are the higher-timeframe ones (4H, daily, weekly, monthly).
IFVG
Inversion FVG: a gap that price broke through, which now works the other way.
OTE
Optimal trade entry: the 62% - 79% pullback of the last move (70.5% is the sweet spot). The pastel gold band.
Pips / R
Pips measure movement (1.34594 to 1.34604 is 1 pip). 2R means the win was twice the amount risked.
Raid / CISD
A raid pushes through a high or low to trigger stops. CISD is the candle close that shows the move has turned.
